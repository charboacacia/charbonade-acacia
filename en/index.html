Welcome

Charbonnade, our specialty

Here, the house specialty is charbonnade: all-you-can-eat grilled meat shared among friends or family. Alongside it, a generous menu running from prawns to fresh pasta, as well as fish and meat. Carolina and Gilberto welcome you with the warmth they brought from their native Bolivia.
